# Privacy policy

*Effective 15 October 2026.*

Git for Me! runs on your own machine. It has no account, no telemetry and no
server of its own, and nothing you do with it reaches the author.

## What leaves your machine

Only read-only searches and lookups, to:

- **GitHub** (`api.github.com`), **GitLab** (`gitlab.com/api`) and
  **Codeberg** (`codeberg.org/api`): searches built from what your agent
  writes for each part of what you want to build (a topic, a language,
  keywords, a few words about the job), and a lookup or two for each repo it
  picks.
- **`GITHUB_TOKEN`**, if you set one, goes only to `api.github.com`, never to
  GitLab or Codeberg, and is never written to a report, a log or a file.

Nothing else is contacted. It reads no files, writes no files and runs no
commands on your machine. [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) has the full detail.

## Third parties

GitHub, GitLab, Codeberg, npm (where it's installed from) and the AI
assistant you use it with each have their own privacy policies.

## Changes

Changes to this policy are made in this file, and its history is in the
